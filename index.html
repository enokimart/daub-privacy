Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Everything you make in Daub stays on your device. The only information that leaves it is what is needed to process and restore a purchase.

What stays on your device

The following are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad, and are never sent anywhere:

  • Photos you open in the app, and the thumbnails Daub saves alongside colors you keep
  • Colors in your book, including the names you give them
  • Your paint palette
  • Settings such as language and appearance

Deleting the app removes all of it. Because this data never leaves your device, we cannot recover it for you, and we cannot see it.

Photos

Daub asks for permission to use your camera and photo library so you can pick a color from a picture. Images are read on your device to sample color values. They are not uploaded, analyzed on a server, or shared. You can withdraw this permission at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases

Daub offers an optional paid upgrade. Payment is taken by Apple through the App Store, and Daub never sees or stores your payment details.

To unlock Pro on your device, and to restore it when you reinstall Daub or sign in on another device, Daub uses RevenueCat, a purchase management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID created by the app, a device identifier, and the status of your purchase. It does not receive your name, your email address, your photos, or anything you make in Daub. This is the only connection Daub makes to a server.

Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy, and RevenueCat's by RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

Daub contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no analytics that follow what you do in the app. It does not build a profile of you, and it does not sell or share your data with other companies. RevenueCat, described above, is the only third party that receives anything from Daub, and only for purchases.

Children

Daub is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

The only record connected to you outside your device is the purchase record described above, held under an anonymous ID. Write to us and we will have it deleted. Everything else lives in the app on your device: you control it directly, and removing the app removes it completely.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Daub handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
