Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Daub does not collect your personal data. Everything you make in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Daub works entirely offline. The following are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad, and are never sent anywhere:

  • Photos you open in the app, and the thumbnails Daub saves alongside colors you keep
  • Colors in your book, including the names you give them
  • Your paint palette
  • Settings such as language and appearance

Deleting the app removes all of it. Because this data never leaves your device, we cannot recover it for you, and we cannot see it.

Photos

Daub asks for permission to use your camera and photo library so you can pick a color from a picture. Images are read on your device to sample color values. They are not uploaded, analyzed on a server, or shared. You can withdraw this permission at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases

Daub offers an optional paid upgrade. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Daub never sees or stores your payment details. Apple may share limited purchase information with us, such as whether a purchase is active, so the app can unlock its paid features. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

Daub contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind. It does not build a profile of you and does not share data with other companies.

Children

Daub is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since Daub holds no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You control all of your data directly in the app, and removing the app removes it completely.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Daub handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
